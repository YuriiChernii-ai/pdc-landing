Meeting formats for everyone

Join the group

Design starts with people. That is why we pay special attention to soft skills: communication, teamwork, presenting ideas. For this we run meetings in several formats.

Nina Voitovych (Design Lead)

Design coffee

A weekly video meeting: you meet an invited speaker or we discuss a chosen topic.

One-on-one design coffee

A one-on-one talk with a random club member: no agenda or presentations, just getting to know each other.

Speaking club

Conversational English in a small group with a moderator, a chosen topic, focus on speaking.

Frequently asked questions

  • Press "Join the group", go to Telegram and join the chat. Write a few words about yourself and we will welcome you to the club.

  • For designers of any level and direction: beginners, mids, seniors and anyone looking for a job or simply wanting to be among peers.

  • Membership is free. Only mentor services are paid; prices are listed in each mentor profile.

  • Open the mentors page, read the profiles and reviews, look at the services. Choose the one whose expertise fits your request and write directly.

  • Once a week in a video chat: random pairs, a conversation with no agenda.

  • Write to us through the "Become a mentor" card on the mentors page: briefly about your experience and direction, and we will agree on the format.

Still have questions?

Write to Yurii and he'll answer if he's in a good mood today; otherwise, beware.
